About

"Dream. Dare. Do."

I’m a Computer Science student at Chung-Ang University, currently spending a semester at Nanyang Technological University after studying at UC Berkeley as a visiting student. I’m interested in 3D computer vision and robotics, and I’m especially drawn to ambitious problems that call for new ideas, deep collaboration, and the courage to explore what hasn’t been done before.

I’m always up for a new challenge, whether it’s in research or somewhere far outside the classroom. I love traveling, meeting new people, and trying new things. I also enjoy football, swimming, golf, and skiing.

On slower days, you might find me at a cozy café, enjoying brunch, a cup of coffee, and a little warm sunlight.
